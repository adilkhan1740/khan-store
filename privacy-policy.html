01

Introduction

Welcome to KHAN Store. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling information responsibly when you use our website and services.

By using KHAN Store, you acknowledge that information may be processed as necessary to provide website functionality, customer support, shopping features and order-related services.

02

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, different types of information may be entered or generated.

Information you provide

  • Name and contact information
  • Email address and phone number
  • Delivery and billing details
  • Messages submitted through contact or support forms
  • Seller application information when you choose to sell through the website

Information generated through use

  • Cart and wishlist information
  • Order information
  • Basic browser and device information
  • Website interaction and preference information
03

How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Providing and improving website functionality
  • Processing and managing orders
  • Providing customer support
  • Responding to contact requests
  • Managing seller applications
  • Protecting the website against misuse or unauthorized activity
04

Cookies & Local Storage

KHAN Store may use browser technologies such as cookies or local storage to remember certain preferences and shopping activity.

Local browser storage

Features such as cart, wishlist and certain website preferences may be stored locally in your browser.

You can manage or clear locally stored browser data through your browser settings. Clearing this information may remove saved shopping data from your device.

05

Orders & Payments

When you place an order, information required for order processing and delivery may be collected, including contact and delivery details.

Payment information should only be submitted through the payment method or payment provider presented by the website. KHAN Store should not require you to send sensitive payment credentials through ordinary contact messages.

Important

Never share your PIN, password, OTP or other confidential authentication information with anyone.

06

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no website, network or electronic storage system can guarantee absolute security.

07

Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party services, platforms, hosting providers, payment services, analytics tools or external resources.

These services may have their own privacy policies and terms. When information is handled by an external provider, its processing may be governed by that provider's applicable policies.

08

Children's Privacy

KHAN Store is intended to provide general shopping and information services. Users should only provide personal information where they are permitted and authorized to do so.

If you believe that personal information has been submitted by a child without appropriate authorization, please contact us so the situation can be reviewed.

09

Your Choices

Depending on the information and applicable requirements, you may have choices regarding your information.

  • Review information you have submitted
  • Request clarification about information handling
  • Clear locally stored browser information
  • Contact support regarding privacy-related questions
10

Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

11

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how information is handled, you can contact the KHAN Store support team.
